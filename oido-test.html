What to try

  1. Tap Preparar and wait for “Listo”.
  2. Tap A · Reproducir, put your earbuds in, and lock the phone. Listen for about a minute.
  3. While it's locked: press the earbud button (pause, play, and double press for next), and try the lock-screen controls.
  4. Unlock, change the speed to 0.8× and 1.2× and lock again for a bit.
  5. Do the same with B · Reproducir.
  6. Tap Copiar el registro and paste it to Claude, with a line on what you heard.
  7. If you can: add this page to the home screen (Share → Add to Home Screen), open it from there, and repeat 2–6. It plays tones there, which is fine for this test.

A · One joined track

Every clip and pause joined into one audio file, so the phone only has to keep one thing playing.

B · Clip by clip

The same items, but the page starts each clip when the last one ends.
